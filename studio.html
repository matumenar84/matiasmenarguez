Studio setup

Built for drums, detail, and feel.

The room is a working studio with real instruments, analog-inspired front end, and a modern digital workflow.

  • Gretsch Round Badge 60s, Gretsch Jazz kit, vintage and modern snares and cymbals.
  • Kultrumm TAB V376, Behringer v1273, Audient ASP880, and Universal Audio Apollo x8p.
  • LUNA, UAD-2, NEVE/API emulators, FabFilter, and professional delivery formats.
